# ColorNinja Studio 1.1.6

## Region Edits in HueForge

- Compatible whole-region edits now appear in HueForge's **SpotFix** list when
  you open an exported HFP.
- Assigning multiple regions to one layer creates one SpotFix group with that
  target height. Height adjustments transfer without being applied twice.
- Overlapping edits resolve to their final result. Pixel selections and other
  groups that cannot transfer exactly keep their finished appearance in the
  image. The companion `.colorninja` project retains the complete editable
  groups, names, protection and history.
- **For existing work, open your ColorNinja project and export a new HFP.**
  Previously exported files do not gain SpotFix entries automatically.

See the [Region Edit guide](https://github.com/fryguy503/ColorNinja/blob/v1.1.6/docs/region-editing.md)
for the supported transfers and fallback behavior.

## Border color and depth

- **Border color** selects from the printable colors in your current filament
  stack and sets the corresponding depth.
- **Border depth** stays visible while matching the image depth. Entering a
  different value switches to a custom depth and updates the predicted color.
- Depth entries snap to the nearest printable layer. With a 0.16 mm first layer
  and 0.08 mm regular layers, **0.86 mm becomes 0.88 mm**.
- Border settings stay with projects and profiles. PNG exports remain image-only.

See the [border guide](https://github.com/fryguy503/ColorNinja/blob/v1.1.6/docs/borders.md).

## Validation

Release checks cover Go and frontend tests, formatting, production builds, Go
vet, dependency audits, compiled-app browser acceptance and extracted packages
on all five platforms. SpotFix compatibility fixtures cover assignment, height
adjustments, clamping, overlap, protection, cut/restore, partial selections,
transparency and footprint orientation. Physical-print and full native-window
acceptance remain separate from these software checks.
